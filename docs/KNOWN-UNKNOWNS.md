# Known unknowns

1. Which chip, display controller, flash layout, and bootloader are present on each physical E87-labeled badge?
2. Does a stock path permit authenticated firmware readback?
3. Which UFW fields are enforced by the device rather than merely by the phone app?
4. What caused the badge UI corruption?
5. Does a custom candidate execute, advertise, drive the panel, or retain recovery?
6. Can the panel remain active while charging without unacceptable battery or thermal effects?
7. What acknowledgement proves end-to-end update success after reboot and reconnect?

Until resolved, this is a research record and design specification—not a deployment guide.
